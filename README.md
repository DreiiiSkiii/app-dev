# My favorite movie: The Croods
## About the Movie
The Croods is an animated adventure-comedy movie released in 2013. It follows a prehistoric family who must leave their cave and explore the outside world after their home is destroyed.
1. Grug – The protective father of the family.
2. Ugga – The caring and supportive mother.
3. Eep – The adventurous daughter who loves exploring.
4. Thunk – The strong but simple-minded son.
5. Sandy – The youngest and wildest member of the family.
6. Guy – A clever young man who helps the family survive.
